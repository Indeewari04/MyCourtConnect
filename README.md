# MyCourtConnect

MyCourtConnect is an academic web application designed to help clients track court cases and connect with lawyers in Sri Lanka. It also supports law students through legal learning resources and internship opportunities.

## Project Objectives

- Help clients access their court case information.
- Simplify lawyer appointment booking and communication.
- Provide court date reminders and notifications.
- Offer free legal documents and a community Q&A space.
- Support law students in finding internship opportunities.

## User Roles

- **Admin:** Manages user profiles and system information.
- **Client:** Views their own cases, books appointments, and communicates with lawyers.
- **Lawyer:** Manages appointments and communicates with clients.
- **Law Student:** Accesses legal resources and internship-related features.

## Main Features

- Role-based registration and login
- Client access to their own court case details
- Lawyer appointment booking
- Court date reminders
- Messaging and notifications
- Free legal documents and community Q&A
- Law student internship module
- Admin management functions
